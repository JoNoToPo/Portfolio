Personal project · Oct 2026 – present

Synth Conductor

A free browser instrument you play with your webcam. Your left hand picks chords and your right hand shapes the sound. No account, no install, no tracking.

Hand tracking runs entirely in the browser with MediaPipe, so the camera image never leaves your computer. It also has Web MIDI output, a looper, a drone, percussion, a modulation matrix and a "Learn the basics" tutorial.

  • JavaScript
  • MediaPipe
  • Tone.js
  • Web Audio
  • Web MIDI
  • GitHub Actions

Made with permission from Eric Wei, creator of Gesture Synth.

Needs a webcam. Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave.

A puzzle game you solve by moving and resizing the game window itself. One box follows the window's width, another follows its left edge. 16 levels, and progress saves automatically.

Built with SOLID design in mind, and checked with Checkstyle, SpotBugs, PMD and JaCoCo in Gradle.

PantryPilot

Our capstone AI project: an agent that turns a photo of a grocery receipt into a pantry with estimated expiry dates, then plans meals that use food before it spoils.

We're in early planning. The plan is to fine-tune a Donut model to read receipts and a YOLO-style detector for loose produce, with a LangGraph multi-agent system that plans meals and replans as food gets used or thrown out.
